In plain terms

What is AL Elite Financing?

AL Elite Financing is a US financing marketplace for home improvement. It matches homeowners who need to pay for a roof, HVAC system or other project with consumer lenders, and matches contractors who need operating capital, equipment or a customer-financing program with business lenders.

AL Elite does not lend money, set interest rates or make credit decisions. Lenders in the network do. AL Elite is paid by lenders and partner contractors, so comparing options costs you nothing.

How matching works

How does AL Elite match you with a lender?

Three steps. You stay in control of which lender, if any, you go ahead with.

Tell us about the project or the business need

Homeowners: the trade, rough cost and your state. Contractors: what you need the capital for, or that you want to offer financing to customers. Takes a couple of minutes.

We match you with lenders who fund that kind of request

Not every lender does roofs. Not every lender does dump trucks. We route your request to the ones that do, and show you what to expect, including whether a lender runs a soft or hard credit check and when.

Compare, choose, and apply directly with the lender

Rates, terms and approval decisions come from the lender. Read their disclosures, pick the offer that fits, and keep your contractor on schedule.

Questions people ask before they apply

Frequently asked questions

Is AL Elite Financing a lender?

No. AL Elite is a financing marketplace and matching service. We connect homeowners and contractors with lenders in our network. The lender sets the rate, fees and term, makes the credit decision and funds the loan. We are paid by lenders and partner contractors, not by you.

Can you finance a roof?

Yes. Roof replacement and repair are among the most common home projects financed with an unsecured personal loan, a contractor-arranged financing plan or a home equity product. Which one makes sense depends on the cost, how fast you need the work done and whether you want to use your home as collateral. Our roof financing page compares the options with monthly payment examples.

Can I get roof or HVAC financing with bad credit?

Some lenders in our network consider applicants with lower credit scores, usually at higher APRs or with a co-applicant. Approval is never guaranteed and we won't tell you otherwise. If your score is below the mid-600s, expect fewer offers and read the APR carefully. See HVAC financing with bad credit and roof financing with bad credit for realistic expectations.

Does checking my options affect my credit score?

Submitting a request to AL Elite does not itself pull your credit. Many lenders in the network show initial options using a soft inquiry, which does not affect your score. A hard inquiry usually happens only when you formally apply with a lender you've chosen. We label each lender's approach before you proceed, so you know which step triggers what.

How much does a new roof cost per month?

As an example, a $15,000 roof financed over 60 months at 9.99% APR with no fees comes to about $319 per month and roughly $4,100 in total interest. Stretch the same amount over 120 months and the payment drops to about $198, but total interest roughly doubles. Use the calculator above to try your own numbers. These are illustrations, not offers.

How do contractors offer financing to customers through AL Elite?

Partner contractors get a financing link and in-home tools to present monthly payment options to customers. The customer applies with a lender from the network; when approved, the lender pays for the work and the customer repays the lender over time. There is no cost to the homeowner for using the matching service, and contractors receive a profile page and a "Financing available through AL Elite" badge. Details and sign-up are on our contractor page.

What kind of business loans can contractors get?

The common requests are working capital to cover payroll and materials between draws, equipment financing for excavators, lifts and tools, dump truck and work truck loans, construction invoice factoring to get paid on slow invoices, and lines of credit. Each is covered under contractor business loans, including what lenders typically want to see, such as time in business, revenue and licensing.

Which states does AL Elite operate in?

We match nationwide in the United States, with dedicated local pages for 20 metros in storm-prone states. Lender availability and loan products vary by state, and some products are not offered in every state. We show you only the lenders who can serve your location.
